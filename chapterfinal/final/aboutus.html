Our Philosophy

Pacific Trails Resort believes that everyone deserves a chance to experience nature. Stunning ocean views, beautifully crafted trails, and immersive surroundings grant you the pleasure of seeing the Earth at its finest. Our goal is to assist you on this breathtaking journey in a manner that doesn't take away from your personal engagement with the local environment. When you stay with us, you are staying with the best in this field.

The Staff at the Pacific Trails Resort are very experienced and will help guide you on your personalized journey. We assure that all amenities and stays are filled with comfort so that you can rest and relax before every experience. Our professionals can guide you an every aspect: from guided tours, to equipment recommendations. Ensuring that you have a spotless experience every time.

Our Values and You

Values are an important part of the process and we strongly believe that the quality of our services matters. We hope that these experiences help you realize the value of nature too.

  • Comfort in a Luxury Yurt helps you focus on enjoying the natural experiences around you.
  • Expansive ocean views and grounded tours help you keep in touch with nature.
  • Healthy choices with delectable tastes assure that you never go hungry while staying with us and have the energy to explore more.
  • Have questions? Our knowledgable staff will help you promptly.
